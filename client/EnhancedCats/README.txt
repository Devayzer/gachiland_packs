 HOW TO ADD CUSTOM CAT MODELS
 ============================
 
 1. MODELS (.geo.json):
    Place them in: assets/enhanced_cats/geo/
    
 2. TEXTURES (.png):
    Place them in: assets/enhanced_cats/textures/entity/
    
 3. IMPORTANT:
    - Do NOT delete the 'pack.mcmeta' file.
    - Ensure your textures name match what the mod expects (e.g. "siamese.png", "calico.png").
    - You may need to restart the game or reload resource packs (F3 + T) to see changes.
